## Clan colours

- Each clan has its own colour from `scripts/clan-palette.json`, 180 colours spread as far apart as possible (OKLab; the first 100, then 80 more between them) that all give the kanji mark a contrast of at least 4.5:1. `npm run data:check` rejects repeats and other colours.
- They were assigned by computer so that clans shown in the same year, and neighbours above all, look as different as possible. They aren't historical. The first 9 (織田, 徳川, 羽柴・豊臣, 後北条, 上杉, 毛利, 池田, 今川, 武田) were chosen earlier by hand.
- For a new clan, use a colour that isn't taken; the check lists those left if given one outside the palette. If a spare is too close to a neighbour's, trade with a clan that isn't shown in any year (done for 高遠, 三村, 丹羽, 蒲生 and 斯波). When 1582 took the place of 1583, 明智, 溝口, 粟屋 and 柴田 were given spares, as they were close to neighbours.
- With this many, some colours look alike, so the kanji mark is still needed to be sure.

## Castle images

- A castle's `image` is a file in `public/image/`, shown at the bottom of its panel with the caption 「AI生成画像」. They are AI generated, not historical. Save them as WebP, 960 px wide (`cwebp -q 80 -resize 960 0`); `npm run data:check` rejects a missing file.
- 伊賀上野城: added 2026-11-26.

## Notes on castle data

- Descriptions are original Japanese text written from the same sources; English is planned with the language toggle (M8).
- Dates are ranges where sources disagree (see ADR-0005). For example, 岐阜城 was abandoned in 1600 or 1601.
- 春日山城: the source only says 南北朝時代, so `built` is 1336–1392.
- 岐阜城: 1201 is the traditional date of the first fort (砦) on the site.
- 躑躅ヶ崎館: Wikipedia classes its structure as 連郭式平城; we use 館.
- 吉田郡山城: the source says the construction date is unknown, so `built` is null (Wikidata gives 1336). `abandoned` is 1591–1600: the Mōri moved to 広島城 in 1591, and other accounts say about 1600. It was demolished after 1615.
- 府内城 and 八王子城: added 2026-11-23 from infobox facts supplied by the project owner, with owners and descriptions from the Japanese Wikipedia articles. 府内城: the article's lead says 平山城, but its infobox and overview say 平城. 八王子城: `built` is 1587 as supplied; the article says 氏照 began it in 1571, or more likely 1578, and made it his base in 1587.
- 岡城: the one in 豊後; its id is `bungo-oka`, as other castles share the name. 1185 is the traditional date. The article also gives a 1334 expansion by 志賀貞朝, but 『豊後国志』 puts the 志賀氏 in 直入郡 only from 1369. The official site is from Wikidata.
- 観音寺城: `built` is 1335–1487: 『太平記』 has a fort there in 1335, and the infobox gives 応仁・文明年間 (1467–1487). Wikidata's 1468 falls within that.
- 小谷城: `built` is 1516–1525: 『浅井三代記』 gives 1516, the article prefers 1523–1524, and it stood by 1525. Wikidata only says the 1520s.
- 佐倉城: `built` is 1532–1610: the infobox suggests 天文年間, but that work was left unfinished, and the article also mentions a 後北条氏 castle of 1585. 土井利勝 began the present castle in 1610. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1873, when the buildings were cleared for the army.
- 弘前城: 高岡城 is the former name until 1628. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1871, though the article says it was probably treated as kept in 1873, and the keep survives.
- 竹田城: the one in 但馬 (たけだ), not 豊後竹田城 (たけた), which is 岡城. `built` is 1431–1443: the infobox gives 1431, and the article's text and Wikidata 1443, both traditional. Coordinates are from the Japanese Wikipedia article, as Wikidata's are given to about 1 km. The official site is the one in both the article and Wikidata.
- 伊賀上野城: the article is 上野城. `built` is 1585–1596: 筒井定次 arrived in 1585, and 『伊水温故』 says it was finished in 文禄年間 (1592–1596). 仁木氏's residence and 滝川雄利's fort stood on the site before. Its official site is http only, so it isn't listed.
- 高遠城: `built` is null, as the article doesn't give it; 1547 is the Takeda rebuilding, per 『甲陽軍鑑』.
- 鹿児島城: `built` is 1601–1604, begun to finished; the infobox gives 1602. 上山城 stood on the hill behind it before. The article is flagged as needing more citations. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1872.
- 二本松城: added as 霞ヶ城, an alternative name it shares with 丸岡城 and 山形城. `built` is 1414–1443: the article gives 1414 or 嘉吉年間 (1441–1443). Wikidata's 1341 is 畠山氏's earlier residence nearby. The official site, by the city's board of education, is from Wikidata.
- 甲府城: `built` is 1583–1585: 家康 is said to have planned it in 1583, and 『甲斐国志』 gives 1585. Most building came later.
- 一乗谷城: part of the article 一乗谷朝倉氏遺跡. `built` is 1336–1392 because the source only says 南北朝時代. Wikidata's coordinates are for the site as a whole, to about 1 km.
- 韮山城: the construction date is uncertain. `built` is 1469–1500: 『北条五代記』 says it was begun in 文明年間 (1469–1486), and it was complete by about 1500.
- 松山城: the one in 伊予; its id is `iyo-matsuyama`, as other castles share the name. The official site was supplied by the project owner.
- 二条城: the Tokugawa castle, which still stands, not the earlier castles of that name (足利, 織田 and 豊臣). `built` is 1601–1603; `abandoned` is null, as it was kept in 1873.
- 和歌山城: Wikidata's coordinates are given to about 1 km.
- 高松城: the one in 讃岐; its id is `sanuki-takamatsu`, as other castles share the name. `built` is 1588 (begun) to 1590 (finished). Wikidata's coordinates are given to about 1 km.

## Notes on castle owners

- Owners come from the same Japanese Wikipedia articles. `from` and `to` are years; in a year of change, the view decides: 1614 (just before 大坂冬の陣, late in the year) shows the last owner; 1553, 1560 and 1600 show the first, just before 川中島 (the first battle), 桶狭間 and 関ヶ原.
- 1582 is just before 本能寺 (2 June), after 武田氏 fell in March. Owners who came in after it that year are marked `after_honnoji`, and the view shows the last of the others. Where the articles give no date, they were marked from the order of events: all of 武田氏's lands had passed to 織田氏 and 徳川氏 by then, and changed hands again only after 信長's death. 1582 took the place of 1583 (its last owners) on 2026-11-25; notes on 1583 are kept.
- In the 1582 view, castles abandoned in 1582 still stand, as `abandoned` has only a year. 那古野城 (perhaps abandoned by 1555) and 鳥越城 (which last fell that March) are shown as 織田氏's. 魚津城 fell to 織田氏 on 3 June, so it is 上杉氏's, and 備中高松城, under siege, is 毛利氏's. 小丸山城, begun that year in an unknown month, is shown as 前田氏's.
- Retainers' castles are listed under the ruling house where the article treats them so (e.g., 甲斐 under 河尻秀隆 is 織田氏). 織田氏 at 清洲城 covers both the 守護代 family and 信長's; 松平氏 is listed as 徳川氏, and 長尾氏 as 上杉氏.
- 小田原城: the Hōjō took it between 1495 and 1501; 1501 is used.
- 春日山城: 上杉氏 (with 長尾氏) is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range.
- 吉田郡山城: 毛利氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period (they held it earlier), to 1600, the end of its abandonment range: they held 安芸 until 関ヶ原, though their base moved to 広島城 in 1591.
- 江戸城: the article's infobox gives 太田氏 for 1561–1577; we list 後北条氏 throughout 1524–1590. Needs checking.
- 大坂城: the Okudaira (松平忠明) years, 1615–1619, are left out. 本願寺 is listed from 1496. 織田氏 in 1580–1582 is the garrison of 丹羽長秀 and 津田信澄; 池田氏 in 1582–1583 is 恒興.
- 府内城: 福原氏 lost it in 1599 and 竹中氏 in 1634 (both years from general knowledge); owners after 1634 are left out.
- 岡城: 志賀氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period (they were retainers of 大友氏), to 1593. The year 1871 for 中川氏 is from the article 岡藩.
- 観音寺城: 六角氏 is listed throughout 1467–1568, although it fell to 京極氏 briefly in 1468 and was given up to the shogun's armies in 1489 and 1491. Possible 織田氏 use after 1568 is left out.
- 小谷城: 浅井氏 is listed from 1525, the end of its construction range; 亮政 fled it for a time in 1538. 豊臣氏 in 1573–1575 is 羽柴秀吉, before he moved to 長浜城. 浅井氏 is marked 井, as 浅野氏 has 浅. The reading あざい is from general knowledge (also あさい).
- 新田金山城: 横瀬氏, who took the name 由良 in 1565, are listed as 由良氏 from 1528. They surrendered to 後北条氏 in 1584 and left in 1585.
- 佐倉城: 千葉氏 is listed from 1555, the end of 天文年間, though the castle was unfinished. Owners in 1590–1610 are left out: the holders of 佐倉 in those years are given in the article 佐倉藩, and they may have used 本佐倉城. Owners after 1633 (the year is from 佐倉藩) are left out.
- 竹田城: 太田垣氏, retainers of 山名氏, are listed from 1443, the end of its construction range. 荻野直正 held it briefly in 1575, left out. 豊臣氏 in 1577–1579 and 1580–1585 is 羽柴秀長, then 桑山重晴. 赤松氏 from 1585 is 赤松広秀 (斎村政広).
- 伊賀上野城: 新庄氏 took it briefly in 1600, left out. Both castellans and lords in 1608–1871 were 藤堂氏; the year 1871 is the infobox's end of the castle.
- 高遠城: 高遠氏 is listed from 1541, the article's first date. 織田氏 in 1582 is uncertain, as the article says its lord then is unknown; 後北条氏's brief hold that year is left out. 保科氏 in 1582–1590 served 徳川氏 (from late 1582). From general knowledge: 1590, when 保科氏 left for 下総; 京極氏 in 1593–1600 (高知, based at 飯田城). 毛利秀頼 in 1590–1593, not of the 毛利氏 of 安芸, is left out, as are owners after 1636 (a year from the article 高遠藩).
- 鹿児島城: the year 1871 for 島津氏 is 廃藩置県.
- 二本松城: 二本松氏 is listed from 1443, the end of its construction range. Castellans are listed under 伊達氏, 蒲生氏, 上杉氏 and 加藤氏; 1627–1628 was 松下氏 under 加藤氏. From general knowledge: 1601 for the change from 上杉氏 back to 蒲生氏, and 1871 for 丹羽氏.
- 甲府城: 加藤氏 (1591–1593) and the year 1593 for 浅野氏 are from general knowledge. 徳川氏 covers 義直 and direct rule; owners after 1616 are left out.
- 一乗谷城: 朝倉氏 is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range. 織田氏 in 1573–1574 is 桂田長俊, 信長's governor (from general knowledge).
- 韮山城: 後北条氏 is listed from 1491, following the article (伊勢盛時). 徳川氏 in 1590–1601 is 家康's retainer 内藤信成.
- 加藤氏 at 甲府城 (光泰) and 松山城 (嘉明) are different families.
- 松山城: owners after 1627 are left out.
- 岡崎城: 今川氏 is listed from 1549, when 松平広忠 died, to 1560. They ruled through castellans; recent research sees the young 竹千代 (家康) as the formal lord.
- 姫路城 in 1560: 黒田氏 were retainers of 小寺氏.
- 和歌山城: 豊臣氏 in 1585–1600 covers 秀長 and the castellans 桑山重晴 and 一晴. 浅野氏 arrived after 関ヶ原.
- 高松城: owners after 1639 are left out.
- 湯築城: 河野氏 is listed throughout 1335–1585, although the castle changed hands at times in the 14th century.
- 月山富田城: 尼子氏 before 1484 were 京極氏's deputies (守護代). 塩冶氏 held it in 1484–1486, left out. 吉川氏 (広家) in 1591–1600 were a branch of 毛利氏.
- 備中松山城: the article gives no dates before 1570. 庄氏 from 1533 and 三村氏 from 1561 are from general knowledge. 徳川氏 in 1600–1617 is the shogunate's 城番; owners after 1641 are left out.
- 岡山城: 金光氏 is listed from 1521, the start of 大永年間. The years 1602 and 1603, and owners after 1632 (still 池田氏), are from general knowledge or left out.
- 新発田城: 新発田氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 溝口氏's end in 1871 is from general knowledge.
- 十河城: 十河氏 is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range, including when 三好氏 were adopted as its heads (一存, 存保). 長宗我部氏 in 1584–1585 is 親武. 十河氏 got it back in 1585: the article's text says 存之, and the sign at the site 存保; both died at 戸次川 in 1586.
- 盛岡城: 南部氏 is listed from 1598, when building began; they lived at 福岡城 (九戸城) for a time after a fire in 1634. The year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 小高城: 相馬氏 is listed from 1337, when they retook it, to 1611. Their base was 牛越城 in 1597–1603 (dates from the article 相馬義胤; the city says it was their seat around 1600), but 小高城 was still among the castles taken from them in 1602 (article 相馬中村藩), so 1600 shows 相馬氏. The confiscation was reversed within the year, so it is left out.
- 久保田城: 佐竹氏 is listed from 1603, when building began. The year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 土崎湊城: 安東氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and covers the 湊 and 檜山 branches, joined in 1570, and 秋田氏, the name taken by 実季. Its brief capture by 豊島氏's side in 1589 is left out. 佐竹氏 entered in 1602.
- 山形城: 最上氏 is listed from 1357, the end of its construction range, as 斯波氏 of 出羽 took that name. Owners after 1643 are left out.
- 葛尾城: 村上氏 is listed from 1501, the first date found for them there. They lost it in April 1553, retook it that month and left it for good in August. Later years are inferred from who held the district (埴科郡 and 川中島), as little is known of the castle itself: 武田氏 to 1582, 上杉氏 from 1582 (article 山浦景国), and 森氏 from 1600, whose castellan held it that year. 織田氏 in 1582 is 森長可, who was given 埴科郡 and three other districts that March (article 甲州征伐). 田丸氏 in 1598–1600 is left out. The years 1598 (上杉氏's move to 会津) and 1603 (森氏's to 美作) are from general knowledge. 森氏 (忠政) isn't 毛利氏.
- 高山城: 金森氏 is listed from 1588, when building began. 前田氏 of 加賀 kept it for the shogunate in 1692–1695, left out.
- 金沢城: 本願寺 in 1546–1580 is the 加賀一向一揆. 織田氏 in 1580–1583 is 佐久間盛政. The year 1871 for 前田氏 is 廃藩置県.
- 七尾城: 畠山氏 is listed from 1429, the end of its construction range. 温井氏 and 三宅氏, who drove out 上杉氏's castellan in 1579, are left out. 織田氏 is listed from 1580, when 長連龍 took it (articles 長連龍 and 三宅長盛), and is 菅屋長頼 in 1581; 前田利家 was given 能登 that August. In 1583 it was held by 前田氏's castellan 安勝. 畠山氏 of 二本松 is listed as 二本松氏.
- 浜松城: 今川氏 is listed from 1514, when 氏親 gave it to 飯尾氏, their retainers (in revolt by 1565). 吉良氏 before that is left out, as are owners after 1600.
- 駿府城: 今川氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 中村氏 (一氏, then 一忠) left after 関ヶ原. 徳川氏 in 1601–1869 covers 内藤信成, 家康, 頼宣, 忠長, the shogunate and the 駿府藩 of 1868.
- 那古野城: 織田氏 is listed to 1582 and covers 信秀, 信長, 信光 and 林秀貞. 今川氏 is 氏豊. The capture was long dated 1532; the article and the official site give 1538.
- 大河内城: 大河内氏, a branch of 北畠氏, are listed as 北畠氏 from 1467, the start of the app's period. 織田氏 in 1569–1576 is 信雄, who became 北畠氏's heir.
- 雑賀城: 鈴木氏, its builders and only known lords, are listed from 1467, the earliest it can have been built. They led the 雑賀衆, allies of 本願寺.
- 室町御所: the shoguns were often away from Kyoto; 足利氏 is listed throughout 1381–1559.
- 勝瑞城: 細川氏 is listed from 1363, the end of its construction range. 三好氏 in 1552–1582 covers 実休, 長治 and 十河存保. Its capture by 細川真之's side in 1577 and the brief occupation in 1580 are left out.
- 多聞山城: 織田氏 in 1573–1576 covers the garrisons and 塙直政.
- 信貴山城: no owner is listed for 1542–1559, as the article calls it a gap. 1559 is the traditional date for 松永久秀; some scholars and the article 筒井順慶 give November 1560. Its brief captures in 1563 and 1568 are left out.
- 筒井城: 筒井氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, although the castle fell to 越智氏 or 古市氏 in 1477, 1483 and 1516. The article 松永久秀 says he took it in August 1559, the article 筒井城の戦い that he made no serious attack then; 筒井氏 is listed until 1565, when he drove out 順慶. From 1568 to 1571 it was 松永氏's again.
- 郡山城: owners before 1580 are left out: it was held by the 郡山衆, who changed sides often. 増田氏 is 長盛. 徳川氏 in 1600–1615 is 大久保長安 and other officials, and 筒井氏 placed there by 家康 in 1614–1615. Owners after 1619 are left out.
- 神辺城: 山名氏 is listed from 1443; their lords before 理興 are mostly unknown. 大内氏 in 1549–1552 is the castellan 青景隆著. 杉原氏 is listed from 1552, when 杉原豊後守 went over to 大内氏, following recent research (articles 杉原盛重 and 神辺合戦); older accounts have 山名理興 return in about 1554 and 杉原盛重 follow him in 1557. They served 毛利氏 from 1554. 藤井皓玄's brief capture is left out. 毛利氏 in 1584–1600 is direct rule, then 元康, who moved to 王子山城 in 1598.
- 三原城: 毛利氏 in 1597–1600: 三原 went back to the main house when 隆景 died (article 毛利輝元). 浅野氏 from 1619 are the 三原 branch, chief retainers of 広島藩; the year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 岡豊城: 長宗我部氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. It fell in 1508 to 本山氏 and their allies; 1511, when 兼序 came back, follows recent research, and the traditional date is 1518, under 国親.
- 朝倉城: 本山氏 is marked 茂, as 本多氏 has 本 (from 清茂 and 茂辰).
- 宇和島城: 西園寺氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and covers their retainers 家藤氏 and 西園寺宣久. They gave in to 大友氏 for a time in 1560. 戸田氏 in 1587–1594 is the castellan of 戸田勝隆, whose base was 大洲城. 藤堂氏 in 1600–1608 is the castellan 良勝. 徳川氏 in 1613–1614 is direct rule by the shogunate.
- 中村城: 一条氏 is listed from 1468, when 教房 arrived. Their retainers 為松氏 are said to have built it. 長宗我部氏 in 1574–1600 is first 吉良親貞; later years are inferred, as they held 土佐 until 関ヶ原. 山内氏 (marked 康, from 康豊) is a branch; 1601 is from general knowledge.
- 黒瀬城: 西園寺氏, its only lords, are listed from 1551, the earliest it can have been built. 戸田氏 in 1587–1594 is the castellan 岩城氏; no owner is listed after 戸田勝隆 died.
- 大垣城: owners before 1544 are left out, as sources differ. 斎藤氏 in 1549–1567 covers 竹腰氏 and, from 1559, 氏家氏, their retainers; 氏家氏 went over to 織田氏 in 1567 (from general knowledge). 豊臣氏 in 1585–1590 is 一柳直末, who served 秀次. 伊藤氏 is marked 盛, from 盛景 and 盛宗. 西軍's garrison in 1600 and owners after it are left out.
- 郡上八幡城: 遠藤氏 is marked 慶, from 慶隆. They are listed to 1588, when 稲葉貞通 came; the article says only that 慶隆 was driven out after 本能寺. 1692 and later owners (井上氏, 金森氏, 青山氏) are from general knowledge or left out.
- 岩村城: 遠山氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1572, and includes the months under 織田信長's son and おつやの方. 織田氏 in 1575–1582 is 河尻秀隆, then 団忠正. 森氏 in 1582–1599 is 長可 and 忠政. 田丸氏 is marked 丸, as 田中氏 has 田. Owners after 1600 are left out.
- 若桜鬼ヶ城: 矢部氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 尼子氏 in 1575–1576 is 山中幸盛 and the restoration army. Owners in 1576–1581, after 吉川元春 took it, are left out. 木下氏 is 重堅's family, marked 堅, and 山崎氏 is marked 崎.
- 米子城: 山名氏 in 1562–1569 held it with 毛利氏's help. 毛利氏 in 1569–1591 is 吉川元春's castellans 福頼元秀 and 古曳吉種. 中村氏 is the family of 駿府城, 加藤氏 that of 甲府城. 池田氏 from 1617 covers 光政 and 光仲 and their castellans (荒尾氏 from 1632).
- 浜田城: owners after 1648 are left out.
- 備中高松城: 石川氏, retainers of 三村氏, are listed from 1561, when 三村氏 is listed at 備中松山城; who held it before isn't known. 毛利氏 in 1575–1582 is 清水宗治. 宇喜多氏 from 1582 is 花房正成. 花房職秀, from 1600, is left out.
- 新高山城: 小早川氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, although their base was 高山城 until 1552.
- 大内氏館, 高嶺城: 大内氏 in 1551–1557 is 義長. 毛利氏 at 高嶺城 is the castellan 市川経好 and others.
- 若山城: 陶氏, retainers of 大内氏 until 1551 and the power behind them after, are listed from 1470, when 弘護 fortified it.
- 勝山城: 大内氏 is their retainers 内藤氏. No owner is listed after 1557.
- 櫛崎城: 毛利氏 is 内藤隆春, listed from 1557, when 大内氏 fell (that he served 毛利氏 then is from general knowledge), then 毛利秀元.
- 立花山城: 立花氏, a branch of 大友氏, are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1568, when 立花鑑載 fell after rebelling against 大友氏, and again from 1571, when 戸次鑑連 (道雪) took the name (article 立花道雪). 大友氏 and 毛利氏 in 1568–1571 are left out. 小早川氏 to 1600 includes 秀秋, whose base was 名島城.
- 柳川城: 1581 for 龍造寺氏, who had 蒲池鎮漣 killed, 1587 for 立花宗茂, 1601–1620 for 田中氏 (the family of 岡崎城) and 1620 are from general knowledge.

- 飫肥城: 島津氏 is listed from 1458 and covers their retainers 新納氏 and the 豊州家 branch. 伊東氏 of 日向 is marked 東, as 伊藤氏 is a different family. The owner in 1587–1588 is left out.
- 小倉城: 高橋氏 is 鑑種 and 元種; the 毛利氏 who built it are left out. 毛利氏 (勝信), originally 森氏, isn't the family of 安芸, and is marked 勝. 小笠原氏, from 1632, is left out.
- 知覧城: 島津氏 is their branch 佐多氏, listed from 1467, the start of the app's period.
- 志布志城: 島津氏 in 1467–1562 covers the branches 新納氏 and, from 1538, 豊州家 (articles 新納氏 and 島津忠朝); 1562 is from the article 肝付兼続.

- 延岡城: 高橋氏 is 元種, of 小倉城. Owners after 1613 are left out.
- 忍城: 成田氏 is marked 泰, from 長泰 and 泰季. They sided with 上杉氏 in 1559–1561 and later 後北条氏. 徳川氏 in 1590–1600 is 松平忠吉; that he left in 1600 is from general knowledge, and later owners are left out.
- 本佐倉城: 千葉氏 is listed from 1484. 徳川氏 in 1590–1603 is direct rule, then 松平忠輝. 小笠原吉次 is left out; 土井氏 is listed from 1610.
- 吉田城: owners before 1546 are left out, as 牧野氏, 戸田氏 and 松平氏 took it from each other often. 徳川氏 in 1565–1590 is 酒井忠次. Owners after 1601 are left out.

- 要害山城: as at 躑躅ヶ崎館, 織田氏 in 1582 is 河尻秀隆 (from general knowledge). 加藤氏 is 光泰; its years and 浅野氏 in 1593–1600 follow 甲府城.

- 若松城: 蘆名氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 加藤氏 is 嘉明's family and 保科氏 that of 高遠城, later 松平氏.

- 箕輪城: 長野氏 is marked 業, from 業正. 井伊氏 in 1590–1598 is 直政.
- 小諸城: 大井氏, before 1554, are left out, as the article doesn't say when 武田氏 took it. 織田氏 in 1582 is 滝川一益's deputy 道家正栄. 徳川氏 in 1582–1590 is 依田氏. Owners after 1622 are left out.

- 松坂城: 服部一忠, 1590–1595, is left out. 徳川氏 from 1619 is 紀州藩.
- 彦根城: 井伊氏 is listed from 1603, when building began.
- 伏見城: 徳川家康 held it from 1599 on 豊臣氏's behalf. It fell to 西軍 in August 1600, between the year's start and 関ヶ原; the 1600 view shows 徳川氏.
- For the 1614 view, from the articles of the domains (浜松藩, 大垣藩, 岩村藩, 三河吉田藩, 飯山藩, 延岡藩) and of 花房職秀: 松平氏 (諸家) stands for the branch families 桜井 (浜松城), 大給 (岩村城), 竹谷 and 深溝 (吉田城) and 久松 (掛川城). 水野氏 at 浜松城 (重央, from general knowledge) is listed with 郡山城's. 石川氏 (三河) at 大垣城 is 康通, 家成 and 忠総. 堀氏 at 飯山城 is 直寄, of 春日山城's 堀氏's chief retainers. 忍城 is listed as 徳川氏 to 1633, as no lord is given between 1600 and then. 皆川広照 at 飯山城, 1603–1609, is left out.

- 大洲城: 宇都宮氏 of 伊予 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1573, for the article's 天正初年, when 大野直之 drove them out. 1587, 1594 and 1595 follow 宇和島城. 加藤氏 is 光泰's family, and 戸田氏 勝隆's.
- 北ノ庄城: 堀氏 in 1585–1598 and 小早川秀秋 in 1598–1599 are from general knowledge. 松平氏 from 1601 is the 越前 branch. 青木氏 is marked 矩, from 一矩.
- 丸岡城: 柴田氏 is 勝豊, then 安井家清. 丹羽氏 in 1583–1585 is 青山宗勝, listed in his own name after. 松平氏 in 1600–1612 is 今村盛次, for 結城秀康 and 松平忠直. 本多氏 is 成重's family, listed with 岡崎城's.
- 鳥越城: 本願寺 is the 山内衆. 織田氏 in 1580–1582 held it with interruptions.
- 富山城: 上杉謙信 took it in spring 1560, before 桶狭間 (the month is from general knowledge), so 神保氏 is listed only to 1559 for the 1560 view to be right. It changed hands often in 1560–1578; 上杉氏 is listed throughout. 神保長住 was driven out in March 1582 by 上杉氏's allies, and 佐々成政 became lord when 織田氏 took it back that month. 佐々氏 to 1587 is from 魚津城's article; 前田氏 is listed from 1595, when they were given 新川郡.
- 増山城: 神保氏 is listed from 1543, when they are known at 富山城; the 一向一揆 held it in 1572. 佐々氏 in 1581–1585 is 佐々成政's men, and 前田氏 中川光重 and others.
- 魚津城: 椎名氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1568, when 椎名康胤 turned against 上杉氏 (from general knowledge). 織田氏 held it for days in June 1582, left out.
- 丹波亀山城: 豊臣氏 in 1582–1595 is the two 秀勝 and 秀秋. 前田氏 (玄以) from 1595 to 1602 is from general knowledge, and is listed as his own, not 加賀's. The owner in 1602–1609 is left out.
- 八上城: 明智氏 in 1579–1582 is 明智光忠. 前田氏 (玄以) in 1602–1608 is 茂勝. Owners in 1582–1602 are left out.
- 黒井城: 赤井氏 is marked 荻, from 荻野, the name 直正 took. 1554, when he killed 荻野秋清, is from general knowledge; 荻野氏 before it is left out. 明智氏 is 斎藤利三. 堀尾氏 is listed to 1584; the lord after 関ヶ原 (川勝秀氏) is left out.
- 高槻城: 入江氏 is listed from 1527, the first record, and served 三好氏. 豊臣氏 in 1585–1600 covers deputies and 新庄直頼, and 徳川氏 in 1600–1615 deputies and 青山忠成.
- 伊丹城: 伊丹氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and is marked 親, from 親興 (from general knowledge).
- 後瀬山城: 朝倉氏 is listed in 1568–1573, after they took 武田元明 away. 1585 for the end of 丹羽氏 and 1587–1593 for 浅野氏 are from general knowledge; 1594 is from 木下利房. 木下氏 (家定) is 勝俊, and 京極氏 高次 and 忠高; 1634 is from general knowledge.
- 国吉城: 粟屋氏 served 若狭武田氏. 豊臣氏 in 1583–1585 is 木村定光; his end isn't given. 木下氏 and 京極氏 are inferred from 後瀬山城, as they were given the district.
- 高浜城: 堀尾氏 in 1583–1585 and 山内氏 in 1585 are from their articles; owners in 1585–1594 are left out. 京極氏 is inferred from 後瀬山城.
- 上月城: owners before 1557 are left out, as 赤松氏 and 山名氏 fought over it. 赤松氏 from 1557 is 政元 and 政範. 宇喜多氏 (上月景貞) held it briefly between 羽柴秀吉's two attacks, left out: the castle's article puts this before 尼子勝久 came, the battle's after. 毛利氏 in 1578 is the siege that ended it; neither article says who held the site afterwards.
- 桜洞城, 松倉城: 三木氏 of 飛騨 (みつき) took the name 姉小路 in 1560; both names are in one entry, marked 姉. They are listed at 桜洞城 from 1516, when 直頼 became head (article 三木重頼), although it isn't known when he built it. The castellan 信綱 was killed in 1579 or 1583, and later lords aren't known. 松倉城 passed to 秀綱 (article 姉小路秀綱). 金森氏 at 桜洞城 in 1585 is the attack that ended it.
- 沼田城: 沼田氏 fell out among themselves in 1558, and 後北条氏's 康元 was in the castle by August 1559. 上杉謙信 took it in autumn 1560, after 桶狭間 (the month is from general knowledge), so the 1560 view shows 後北条氏. In 1578–1580, 後北条氏 is their castellans 猪俣邦憲 and 金子泰清. 武田氏 in 1580–1582 is 真田昌幸's men, who took it for them, and 織田氏 in 1582 is 滝川益重. 真田氏 from 1582 is listed in their own name, as they changed sides often. Owners after 1681 (the shogunate, then 本多氏, 黒田氏 and 土岐氏) are left out.
- 岸和田城: owners before 1549 (the 和泉 branch of 細川氏) are left out, as it's unclear whether they held this castle or the older one. 松浦氏 of 和泉, deputy governors, read まつら and marked 浦, are listed from 1549, when the article's table has 松浦守 become lord, though his base was 堺 (article 松浦守). 三好氏 in 1558–1562 is 実休, 十河一存 and 安宅冬康, acting as guardians of the young 松浦氏 heir; some date their entry to 1560. 細川氏's brief return in 1562 is left out. 松浦氏 to 1577 includes 寺田 (松浦) 宗清, who killed 松浦光 in 1575. 織田氏 in 1577–1583 is 織田信張, then 蜂屋頼隆; 宗清 stayed on and helped defend it in 1584. 中村氏 is the family of 駿府城, and 岡部氏 that of 丹波亀山城 (from general knowledge). 小出氏 in 1585–1619 is marked 出. The castle's article has 松平信吉 and 北条氏重 hold it in 1614, but the domain's article has 小出吉英 throughout, so 小出氏 is shown in the 1614 view. 松平氏 in 1619–1640 is 康重 (and 康映 for weeks).
- 飯盛城: 木沢氏 is listed from 1531, when it was first besieged, to 1542, when 長政 fell at 太平寺 (the year is from general knowledge). 安見氏, retainers of 畠山氏 of 河内, marked 見, are listed from 1551, when 宗房 is known there (article 安見宗房); who held it in 1542–1551 is left out, though the castle's article goes straight to 宗房. He was driven out in 1559 and put back by 畠山高政. 三好長慶 entered in the eleventh month of 1560, so the 1560 view shows 安見氏. 三好氏 is listed to 1568, when 織田信長 took 河内 (from general knowledge); 畠山秋高 and 遊佐氏, to 1576, are left out.
- 芥川山城: 細川氏 in 1516–1549 covers 高国 and his castellans 能勢氏, 晴元 (from 1527, resident from 1533), 薬師寺元房 and others; the few weeks 三好長慶 held it in 1539 are left out. 三好氏 from 1549 includes 芥川孫十郎, said to be of a 三好 branch (article 芥川孫十郎), who rebelled in 1552, came back that December, and rebelled again in July 1553; 長慶 took the castle that August or September. It also includes 義興 and 長逸. 和田氏 in 1568–1573 is 惟政 and 惟長, whose seat was 高槻城 from 1569; 高山氏 kept it for them, and are not listed.
- 三木城: 別所氏's loss of it in 1530–1531 is left out. 豊臣氏 in 1580–1585 is 杉原家次 and 前野長康, and in 1594–1600 deputies, who aren't known well. 中川氏 in 1585–1594 is 秀政 and 秀成, who went to 岡城. 池田氏 from 1600 is the castellans 伊木忠次 and 忠繁.
- 岩屋城: 高橋氏, retainers of 大友氏, are the clan of 小倉城. 鑑種 is listed from 1559 (article 高橋鑑種), when he was given 宝満城, to July 1567, when 大友氏 took the castle after he rebelled; earlier holders, and 大友氏 in 1567–1569, are left out. 鎮種 (紹運), who was made head of the family in his place in 1569 (article 高橋紹運), held it to 1586. 島津氏 in 1586 is the siege that ended it.
- 三星城: 後藤氏 of 美作 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period; they held it for about 200 years to 1579 (article 後藤勝基). They served 尼子氏 until about 1560, then worked with 浦上氏. 宇喜多氏 in 1579 is the siege that ended it.
- 松ヶ島城: 織田氏 in 1580–1584 is 信雄, then his retainer 津川義冬, put in charge after 本能寺 (article 津川義冬) and killed on 信雄's orders in March 1584; that he was there at the end of 1583 is inferred. 滝川雄利, who took the castle over for 信雄 that year, gave it up to 羽柴秀長 after a siege (article 滝川雄利); 秀長's brief hold is left out. 蒲生氏 is 氏郷, given the castle after the war, though he is placed there by August 1584 (article 蒲生氏郷).
- 坂本城: 丹羽氏 in 1582–1583 is 長秀, given the district at 清洲 in 1582 (article 丹羽長秀). 豊臣氏 in 1583 is 杉原家次, 秀吉's kinsman, not of the 杉原氏 of 備後; he became lord in August (article 杉原家次), and a diary has him there on 20 November. 浅野氏 is 長政, who moved in from 瀬田城 that November and issued rules for the town in December (article 浅野長政), so the 1583 view shows 浅野氏. Article 杉原家次 gives no end to his time there.
- 田辺城: owners are from the chronology in the city's report on its 32nd dig. 細川氏 in 1581–1600 is 藤孝 (幽斎), who lived here after retiring, in 忠興's domain; 幽斎 held it through the 1600 siege (21 July to 12 September, ended by the emperor's envoys), so the 1600 view shows 細川氏. 京極氏 was granted 丹後 in November 1600; 高知 entered in March 1601. From 1622 the 田辺 domain was 高三's line, which ended here when 高盛 was moved to 豊岡 in 1668. 牧野氏 in 1668–1871, from 親成, are the family of 三河 (article 三河牧野氏), read まきのし without the place, and marked 牧. 一色氏, of the older 守護 seat at 八田, isn't listed, as the castle came later. Who held it for the 西軍 after the surrender is left out.
- 弓木城: 稲富氏, retainers of 丹後一色氏, held it as their seat; they are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and marked 祐 (from 祐直), as 稲葉氏 has 稲 and 富田氏 富. 一色氏 is 丹後一色氏, marked 色. 義定 took it over in 1579, after 一色義道 died (from 建部山城, per the tourist association), held it against 細川藤孝 and 忠興, and made peace by marrying 藤孝's daughter; the 一色義定 article dates the peace to 1579 and 1580, and the marriage to 1580 and 1581. Its 生涯 section is flagged as lacking sources and notes he may be known only from 『一色軍記』. He was killed at 宮津城 on 8 September 1582 (some sources say February), after 本能寺, so the 1582 view shows 一色氏, and 細川氏 is `after_honnoji`. His uncle 一色義清 took it back briefly and was killed; that is left out. 稲富祐直, the gunner, later served 細川忠興; when he left the castle is unknown.
- 太田城: 太田氏 of 紀伊, the 宮郷衆 (太田党) of the 雑賀 area, are listed from 1576, when 太田左近 is said to have repaired or built the castle; they're marked 左, as 太田氏 (of 道灌) has 太, and read おおたし from general knowledge. 左近 isn't named in sources of the time; the 和歌山市史 suggests 太田源三大夫 or 太田次郎佐衛門. 紀氏, the builders, and owners before 1576 are left out, as the article gives no dates. 宮郷衆 sided with 織田氏 in 1577, and held it against 雑賀衆 in 1578 with help from 根来衆. 豊臣氏 in 1585 is 羽柴秀吉's siege: from 24 March, the river let in on 1 April; it surrendered on 22 April (the date of 秀吉's 朱印状 and the article's battle box) or 24 April (the text), when 左近 and 53 others killed themselves.
- 小田城: 小田氏 of 常陸 (read おだし, marked 治 from 氏治, as 小早川氏 has 小 and 田中氏 田; not 織田氏) is listed from 1185. 氏治 lost and retook it many times from the 1550s; those losses, including to 上杉謙信 in 1564, are left out. 佐竹氏 is listed from 1569, the year of 手這坂 in some accounts, as 太田資正 is said to have been its castellan for them from 1570 and his son 梶原政景 from 1572; the article's summary gives 1573 for the final loss. 小場義宗, a relative of 佐竹氏, held it later.
- 水戸城: 大掾氏 (馬場氏), before 1427, are left out. 江戸氏 (marked 江) is listed from 1427, when 江戸通房 seized it while 大掾満幹 was away; he had beaten 満幹 in 1416. 佐竹氏 is listed from 1590, the article's summary, though its history section has 義重 and 義宣 drive out 江戸重通 in 1594; so the 1600 view shows 佐竹氏. 徳川氏 from 1602 is 武田信吉 (a son of 家康), 徳川頼宣 from 1603, and the 水戸徳川家 from 頼房 in 1609 to 1871.
- 太田城 (常陸): 佐竹氏 is listed from 1335 (建武2年11月, per a study cited in the article), when they regained it; they had held it from the 12th century until 源頼朝 took the area. The 山入一揆, when 山入氏 held it for a time before 1504 (from general knowledge), is left out. They moved their seat to 水戸城 after 1590, but kept the castle until 1602.
- 神戸城: 神戸氏 (marked 澄, from 盛澄, the founder, as 神保氏 has 神 and 戸田氏 戸) is listed from 1555, the end of the construction range. 具盛 (the 7th head) made peace with 織田信長 in 1568 by adopting his son 信孝; 織田氏 is listed from 1571, when 具盛 was confined at 日野城, as 信孝, who came of age and took over in 1572, went back to the name 織田 in 1582. 織田氏 to 1590 also covers 信雄's men: his chief retainer 林与五郎 (who took the name 神戸) after 信孝's death in 1583, and 滝川雄利 from 1585 (article 神戸藩). 蒲生氏郷 drove out 与五郎 in 1584; that brief hold is left out. 豊臣氏 in 1590–1600 is 雄利, who stayed on after 信雄 was banished, with 水野忠重 for a time; 雄利 sided with the 西軍 and lost it in 1600. 一柳氏 (marked 柳) is 直盛, 1601–1636; 徳川氏 in 1636–1651 is the shogunate's direct rule. 石川氏 (三河) is 総長, a son of 石川忠総, and his line; 本多氏 is 忠統 and his line, to 1871.
- 高岡城: 前田氏 is 利長, who retired here from 富山城 and died in 1614, so the 1614 view shows 前田氏. After 1615 the 加賀 domain kept stores and a guard post on the site, not listed.
- 浜田城: 田原氏 of 伊勢 (marked 綱, from 元綱 and 重綱, as 田中氏 has 田 and 福原氏 原), one of the three 赤堀 houses, are listed from 1470; the reading たわらし is from general knowledge. Article 赤堀氏 says they served 北畠氏 and allied with 神戸氏. 織田氏 in 1575 is 滝川一益's attack, in which 元綱 was killed; 『伊勢軍記』 gives no year, which is from 四日市市教育委員会. 滝川雄利's stand in 1584, for 織田信雄, is left out, as the board has the castle abandoned in 1575.
- 鉢形城: 長尾景春, who built it in revolt against 山内上杉氏, held it until 太田道灌 took it in 1478; he is left out, as listing him as 上杉氏 would be wrong. 上杉氏 in 1478–1525 is 山内上杉氏: 顕定, 顕実, and 憲房, who took it in 1512 and died in 1525. Who held it from 1525 to 1564 is left out, as the article doesn't say; 藤田氏, whose heir 北条氏邦 became, were based at 天神山城 and 用土城 (article 藤田康邦). 後北条氏 is listed from 1564, when 氏邦 entered it, though 1569 is held more likely by the works the article cites. 豊臣氏 in 1590 is the siege by 前田利家, 上杉景勝 and others, after which it was given up.
- 古宮城: 武田氏 in 1571–1575 includes the castellans named in 『二葉松』 (小幡, 甘利 and 大熊). In 1573 奥平氏, who had left 武田氏 for 徳川家康, set fires near it to stop its garrison chasing them.
- 津城: 細野氏, a branch of 長野氏, held it before 1568; they are left out, as the article gives no years. 織田氏 in 1568–1594 is 津田一安, then 信包 from 1569, who had been adopted by 長野氏; he was moved to 丹波 in 1594. 富田氏 is 一白, from July 1595, and 信高, who held it through the 1600 siege, so the 1600 view shows 富田氏. 藤堂氏 is 高虎, from 1608, and his line, to 1871.
- 田丸城: 田丸氏, a branch of 北畠氏 (from 顕晴, a son of 政郷), are listed as 北畠氏 from 1467, the start of the app's period. 織田氏 from 1575 is 信雄, then 田丸直昌 as his man; the castle's article has 信雄 leave for 松ヶ島城 after a fire in 1580, but 田丸藩 has him move to 大河内城, with 直昌 back. 蒲生氏 in 1584–1590 is 直昌 under 蒲生氏郷 of 松ヶ島城. 稲葉氏 from 1590 is 稲葉重通 as castellan for his son 牧村利貞 (article 田丸藩), then 道通 (from 1593 at 岩出城, and here from 1600) and 紀通, to 1616. 藤堂氏 in 1617–1619 is 高虎's deputy. 徳川氏 from 1619 is the 紀州 domain, with 久野氏 as castellans, to 1871.
- 河後森城: 渡辺氏 of 伊予, read わたなべし and marked 渡, are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, as it was their seat for generations. 長宗我部氏 from 1580 is 芝源三郎, 渡辺教忠's attendant, who took it for them in 1580 or 1581. The article dates 戸田勝隆's arrival to after 四国の役 of 1583 (天正11年), but that campaign was in 1585 (from general knowledge); 小早川氏 in 1585–1587 and 戸田氏 in 1587–1594 follow 宇和島城. 藤堂氏 in 1595–1608 and 富田氏 in 1608–1613 are also from 宇和島城, as the article only says 高虎 moved its keep there. 伊達氏 is 秀宗's retainer 桑折 (石母田) 景頼, from 1614.
- 鷹取城: 筑紫氏, a branch of 少弐氏, held it from 1345 for some generations; they are left out, as the article gives no end. 大友氏 (marked 友) is listed from 1542, when their retainer 毛利 (森) 鎮実, who had gone over to 大内氏, surrendered to 大友義鎮; he held it for them after. The 1587 change to 小早川氏 is inferred from 隆景 being given 筑前, as the article says the details are unknown. 黒田氏 from 1601 is 母里友信, then 手塚光重 from 1606.
- 能島城: 能島村上氏 (marked 能; not the 村上氏 of 信濃 at 葛尾城), the head of the three 村上 houses, are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period; the text calls them 能島水軍 (野島氏). They served 大内氏, then 毛利氏; 小早川隆景 besieged the island in 1571–1572, when 武吉 turned against 毛利氏 for a time, which is left out. 小早川氏 from 1585 is 隆景, who took it after 武吉 refused to give back 来島 and stayed out of the 四国 campaign, and moved him to 竹原 (article 村上武吉); the year is from general knowledge, as the article gives none.
- 丸山城: 北畠氏 in 1576 is 具教, who began it as his place of retirement but went back to 三瀬館 after falling out with 織田信長. 織田氏 in 1578 is 滝川雄利, rebuilding it for 信雄 from March to the attack of 25 October. 織田氏 in 1581–1584 is 雄利 again, made 守護 of 伊賀 after the second 天正伊賀の乱 (『諸国廃城考』, cited in the article); that he held this castle then is a view, not certain.
- 福地城: 福地氏 of 伊賀 (read ふくちし, marked 地, as 福島氏 has 福), one of the 柘植三方, said to descend from 平清盛, are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to June 1582, so the 1582 view shows them. 池尻氏, also named as lords in the infobox, are left out, as no years are given.
- 米沢城: 長井氏 lost it to 伊達宗遠 in 1380, 1403 or 1413 (accounts differ); 伊達氏 are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, though their seat was 桑折西山城 until 1548. 伊達氏 to 1591 includes their castellans 伊達宗澄 and 梁川宗清 in 1589–1590, while 政宗's seat was 黒川城. 蒲生氏 in 1591–1598 is 蒲生郷安, 氏郷's chief retainer. The article dates 上杉氏's arrival to 1597 (慶長2年), but 蒲生秀行 was moved to 宇都宮 in 1598 (慶長3年, from general knowledge), so 1598 is used. 上杉氏 is 直江兼続 as castellan to 1601, then 景勝, whose seat it was, and his line to 1871.
- 赤木城: 豊臣氏 in 1589–1600 is 豊臣秀長's 紀伊, with 藤堂高虎, his retainer and timber commissioner there, building it after the 北山一揆 of 1586–1589; who held it after 秀長 died is left out, and the span follows 和歌山城. 浅野氏 from 1600 is 浅野忠吉 of 新宮城, lord in the 慶長 years (article), under 浅野幸長; that they had it from 1600 is from general knowledge. The second 北山一揆, in December 1614, struck while 忠吉 was at 大坂.
- Known gaps: 岐阜城 before 1533; 岡崎城 before 1531; 躑躅ヶ崎館 after 1590.
- From general knowledge: 大久保氏 at 小田原城 from 1590; that 田中氏 left 岡崎城 in 1601; and clan readings and English names.
