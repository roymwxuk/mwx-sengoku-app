## Clan colours

- Each clan has its own colour from `scripts/clan-palette.json`, 130 colours spread as far apart as possible (OKLab; the first 100, then 30 more between them) that all give the kanji mark a contrast of at least 4.5:1. `npm run data:check` rejects repeats and other colours.
- They were assigned by computer so that clans shown in the same year, and neighbours above all, look as different as possible. They aren't historical. The first 9 (織田, 徳川, 羽柴・豊臣, 後北条, 上杉, 毛利, 池田, 今川, 武田) were chosen earlier by hand.
- For a new clan, use a colour that isn't taken; the check lists those left if given one outside the palette. If a spare is too close to a neighbour's, trade with a clan that isn't shown in any year (done for 高遠, 三村, 丹羽 and 蒲生).
- With this many, some colours look alike, so the kanji mark is still needed to be sure.

## Notes on castle data

- Descriptions are original Japanese text written from the same sources; English is planned with the language toggle (M8).
- Dates are ranges where sources disagree (see ADR-0005). For example, 岐阜城 was abandoned in 1600 or 1601.
- 春日山城: the source only says 南北朝時代, so `built` is 1336–1392.
- 岐阜城: 1201 is the traditional date of the first fort (砦) on the site.
- 躑躅ヶ崎館: Wikipedia classes its structure as 連郭式平城; we use 館.
- 吉田郡山城: the source says the construction date is unknown, so `built` is null (Wikidata gives 1336). `abandoned` is 1591–1600: the Mōri moved to 広島城 in 1591, and other accounts say about 1600. It was demolished after 1615.
- 府内城 and 八王子城: added 2026-11-23 from infobox facts supplied by the project owner, with owners and descriptions from the Japanese Wikipedia articles. 府内城: the article's lead says 平山城, but its infobox and overview say 平城. 八王子城: `built` is 1587 as supplied; the article says 氏照 began it in 1571, or more likely 1578, and made it his base in 1587.
- 岡城: the one in 豊後; its id is `bungo-oka`, as other castles share the name. 1185 is the traditional date. The article also gives a 1334 expansion by 志賀貞朝, but 『豊後国志』 puts the 志賀氏 in 直入郡 only from 1369. The official site is from Wikidata.
- 観音寺城: `built` is 1335–1487: 『太平記』 has a fort there in 1335, and the infobox gives 応仁・文明年間 (1467–1487). Wikidata's 1468 falls within that.
- 小谷城: `built` is 1516–1525: 『浅井三代記』 gives 1516, the article prefers 1523–1524, and it stood by 1525. Wikidata only says the 1520s.
- 佐倉城: `built` is 1532–1610: the infobox suggests 天文年間, but that work was left unfinished, and the article also mentions a 後北条氏 castle of 1585. 土井利勝 began the present castle in 1610. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1873, when the buildings were cleared for the army.
- 弘前城: 高岡城 is the former name until 1628. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1871, though the article says it was probably treated as kept in 1873, and the keep survives.
- 竹田城: the one in 但馬 (たけだ), not 豊後竹田城 (たけた), which is 岡城. `built` is 1431–1443: the infobox gives 1431, and the article's text and Wikidata 1443, both traditional. Coordinates are from the Japanese Wikipedia article, as Wikidata's are given to about 1 km. The official site is the one in both the article and Wikidata.
- 伊賀上野城: the article is 上野城. `built` is 1585–1596: 筒井定次 arrived in 1585, and 『伊水温故』 says it was finished in 文禄年間 (1592–1596). 仁木氏's residence and 滝川雄利's fort stood on the site before. Its official site is http only, so it isn't listed.

- 鹿児島城: `built` is 1601–1604, begun to finished; the infobox gives 1602. 上山城 stood on the hill behind it before. The article is flagged as needing more citations. `abandoned` is the infobox's 1872.
- 二本松城: added as 霞ヶ城, an alternative name it shares with 丸岡城 and 山形城. `built` is 1414–1443: the article gives 1414 or 嘉吉年間 (1441–1443). Wikidata's 1341 is 畠山氏's earlier residence nearby. The official site, by the city's board of education, is from Wikidata.
- 甲府城: `built` is 1583–1585: 家康 is said to have planned it in 1583, and 『甲斐国志』 gives 1585. Most building came later.
- 一乗谷城: part of the article 一乗谷朝倉氏遺跡. `built` is 1336–1392 because the source only says 南北朝時代. Wikidata's coordinates are for the site as a whole, to about 1 km.
- 韮山城: the construction date is uncertain. `built` is 1469–1500: 『北条五代記』 says it was begun in 文明年間 (1469–1486), and it was complete by about 1500.
- 松山城: the one in 伊予; its id is `iyo-matsuyama`, as other castles share the name. The official site was supplied by the project owner.
- 二条城: the Tokugawa castle, which still stands, not the earlier castles of that name (足利, 織田 and 豊臣). `built` is 1601–1603; `abandoned` is null, as it was kept in 1873.
- 和歌山城: Wikidata's coordinates are given to about 1 km.
- 高松城: the one in 讃岐; its id is `sanuki-takamatsu`, as other castles share the name. `built` is 1588 (begun) to 1590 (finished). Wikidata's coordinates are given to about 1 km.

## Notes on castle owners

- Owners come from the same Japanese Wikipedia articles. `from` and `to` are years; in a year of change, the view decides: 1583 shows the last owner; 1553, 1560 and 1600 show the first, just before 川中島 (the first battle), 桶狭間 and 関ヶ原.
- Retainers' castles are listed under the ruling house where the article treats them so (e.g., 甲斐 under 河尻秀隆 is 織田氏). 織田氏 at 清洲城 covers both the 守護代 family and 信長's; 松平氏 is listed as 徳川氏, and 長尾氏 as 上杉氏.
- 小田原城: the Hōjō took it between 1495 and 1501; 1501 is used.
- 春日山城: 上杉氏 (with 長尾氏) is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range.
- 吉田郡山城: 毛利氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period (they held it earlier), to 1600, the end of its abandonment range: they held 安芸 until 関ヶ原, though their base moved to 広島城 in 1591.
- 江戸城: the article's infobox gives 太田氏 for 1561–1577; we list 後北条氏 throughout 1524–1590. Needs checking.
- 大坂城: the Okudaira (松平忠明) years, 1615–1619, are left out. 本願寺 is listed from 1496. 織田氏 in 1580–1582 is the garrison of 丹羽長秀 and 津田信澄; 池田氏 in 1582–1583 is 恒興.
- 府内城: 福原氏 lost it in 1599 and 竹中氏 in 1634 (both years from general knowledge); owners after 1634 are left out.
- 岡城: 志賀氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period (they were retainers of 大友氏), to 1593. The year 1871 for 中川氏 is from the article 岡藩.
- 観音寺城: 六角氏 is listed throughout 1467–1568, although it fell to 京極氏 briefly in 1468 and was given up to the shogun's armies in 1489 and 1491. Possible 織田氏 use after 1568 is left out.
- 小谷城: 浅井氏 is listed from 1525, the end of its construction range; 亮政 fled it for a time in 1538. 豊臣氏 in 1573–1575 is 羽柴秀吉, before he moved to 長浜城. 浅井氏 is marked 井, as 浅野氏 has 浅. The reading あざい is from general knowledge (also あさい).
- 新田金山城: 横瀬氏, who took the name 由良 in 1565, are listed as 由良氏 from 1528. They surrendered to 後北条氏 in 1584 and left in 1585.
- 佐倉城: 千葉氏 is listed from 1555, the end of 天文年間, though the castle was unfinished. Owners in 1590–1610 are left out: the holders of 佐倉 in those years are given in the article 佐倉藩, and they may have used 本佐倉城. Owners after 1633 (the year is from 佐倉藩) are left out.
- 竹田城: 太田垣氏, retainers of 山名氏, are listed from 1443, the end of its construction range. 荻野直正 held it briefly in 1575, left out. 豊臣氏 in 1577–1579 and 1580–1585 is 羽柴秀長, then 桑山重晴. 赤松氏 from 1585 is 赤松広秀 (斎村政広).
- 伊賀上野城: 新庄氏 took it briefly in 1600, left out. Both castellans and lords in 1608–1871 were 藤堂氏; the year 1871 is the infobox's end of the castle.
- 高遠城: 高遠氏 is listed from 1541, the article's first date. 織田氏 in 1582 is uncertain, as the article says its lord then is unknown; 後北条氏's brief hold that year is left out. 保科氏 in 1582–1590 served 徳川氏 (from late 1582). From general knowledge: 1590, when 保科氏 left for 下総; 京極氏 in 1593–1600 (高知, based at 飯田城). 毛利秀頼 in 1590–1593, not of the 毛利氏 of 安芸, is left out, as are owners after 1636 (a year from the article 高遠藩).
- 鹿児島城: the year 1871 for 島津氏 is 廃藩置県.
- 二本松城: 二本松氏 is listed from 1443, the end of its construction range. Castellans are listed under 伊達氏, 蒲生氏, 上杉氏 and 加藤氏; 1627–1628 was 松下氏 under 加藤氏. From general knowledge: 1601 for the change from 上杉氏 back to 蒲生氏, and 1871 for 丹羽氏.
- 甲府城: 加藤氏 (1591–1593) and the year 1593 for 浅野氏 are from general knowledge. 徳川氏 covers 義直 and direct rule; owners after 1616 are left out.
- 一乗谷城: 朝倉氏 is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range. 織田氏 in 1573–1574 is 桂田長俊, 信長's governor (from general knowledge).
- 韮山城: 後北条氏 is listed from 1491, following the article (伊勢盛時). 徳川氏 in 1590–1601 is 家康's retainer 内藤信成.
- 加藤氏 at 甲府城 (光泰) and 松山城 (嘉明) are different families.
- 松山城: owners after 1627 are left out.
- 岡崎城: 今川氏 is listed from 1549, when 松平広忠 died, to 1560. They ruled through castellans; recent research sees the young 竹千代 (家康) as the formal lord.
- 姫路城 in 1560: 黒田氏 were retainers of 小寺氏.
- 和歌山城: 豊臣氏 in 1585–1600 covers 秀長 and the castellans 桑山重晴 and 一晴. 浅野氏 arrived after 関ヶ原.
- 高松城: owners after 1639 are left out.
- 湯築城: 河野氏 is listed throughout 1335–1585, although the castle changed hands at times in the 14th century.
- 月山富田城: 尼子氏 before 1484 were 京極氏's deputies (守護代). 塩冶氏 held it in 1484–1486, left out. 吉川氏 (広家) in 1591–1600 were a branch of 毛利氏.
- 備中松山城: the article gives no dates before 1570. 庄氏 from 1533 and 三村氏 from 1561 are from general knowledge. 徳川氏 in 1600–1617 is the shogunate's 城番; owners after 1641 are left out.
- 岡山城: 金光氏 is listed from 1521, the start of 大永年間. The years 1602 and 1603, and owners after 1632 (still 池田氏), are from general knowledge or left out.
- 新発田城: 新発田氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 溝口氏's end in 1871 is from general knowledge.
- 十河城: 十河氏 is listed from 1392, the end of its construction range, including when 三好氏 were adopted as its heads (一存, 存保). 長宗我部氏 in 1584–1585 is 親武. 十河氏 got it back in 1585: the article's text says 存之, and the sign at the site 存保; both died at 戸次川 in 1586.
- 盛岡城: 南部氏 is listed from 1598, when building began; they lived at 福岡城 (九戸城) for a time after a fire in 1634. The year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 小高城: 相馬氏 is listed from 1337, when they retook it, to 1611. Their base was 牛越城 in 1597–1603 (dates from the article 相馬義胤; the city says it was their seat around 1600), but 小高城 was still among the castles taken from them in 1602 (article 相馬中村藩), so 1600 shows 相馬氏. The confiscation was reversed within the year, so it is left out.
- 久保田城: 佐竹氏 is listed from 1603, when building began. The year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 土崎湊城: 安東氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and covers the 湊 and 檜山 branches, joined in 1570, and 秋田氏, the name taken by 実季. Its brief capture by 豊島氏's side in 1589 is left out. 佐竹氏 entered in 1602.
- 山形城: 最上氏 is listed from 1357, the end of its construction range, as 斯波氏 of 出羽 took that name. Owners after 1643 are left out.
- 葛尾城: 村上氏 is listed from 1501, the first date found for them there. They lost it in April 1553, retook it that month and left it for good in August. Later years are inferred from who held the district (埴科郡 and 川中島), as little is known of the castle itself: 武田氏 to 1582, 上杉氏 from 1582 (article 山浦景国), and 森氏 from 1600, whose castellan held it that year. 織田氏's brief hold in 1582 and 田丸氏 in 1598–1600 are left out. The years 1598 (上杉氏's move to 会津) and 1603 (森氏's to 美作) are from general knowledge. 森氏 (忠政) isn't 毛利氏.
- 高山城: 金森氏 is listed from 1588, when building began. 前田氏 of 加賀 kept it for the shogunate in 1692–1695, left out.
- 金沢城: 本願寺 in 1546–1580 is the 加賀一向一揆. 織田氏 in 1580–1583 is 佐久間盛政. The year 1871 for 前田氏 is 廃藩置県.
- 七尾城: 畠山氏 is listed from 1429, the end of its construction range. 温井氏 and 三宅氏, who drove out 上杉氏's castellan in 1579, are left out. 織田氏 is listed from 1580, when 長連龍 took it (articles 長連龍 and 三宅長盛), and is 菅屋長頼 in 1581; 前田利家 was given 能登 that August. In 1583 it was held by 前田氏's castellan 安勝. 畠山氏 of 二本松 is listed as 二本松氏.
- 浜松城: 今川氏 is listed from 1514, when 氏親 gave it to 飯尾氏, their retainers (in revolt by 1565). 吉良氏 before that is left out, as are owners after 1600.
- 駿府城: 今川氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 中村氏 (一氏, then 一忠) left after 関ヶ原. 徳川氏 in 1601–1869 covers 内藤信成, 家康, 頼宣, 忠長, the shogunate and the 駿府藩 of 1868.
- 那古野城: 織田氏 is listed to 1582 and covers 信秀, 信長, 信光 and 林秀貞. 今川氏 is 氏豊. The capture was long dated 1532; the article and the official site give 1538.
- 大河内城: 大河内氏, a branch of 北畠氏, are listed as 北畠氏 from 1467, the start of the app's period. 織田氏 in 1569–1576 is 信雄, who became 北畠氏's heir.
- 雑賀城: 鈴木氏, its builders and only known lords, are listed from 1467, the earliest it can have been built. They led the 雑賀衆, allies of 本願寺.
- 室町御所: the shoguns were often away from Kyoto; 足利氏 is listed throughout 1381–1559.
- 勝瑞城: 細川氏 is listed from 1363, the end of its construction range. 三好氏 in 1552–1582 covers 実休, 長治 and 十河存保. Its capture by 細川真之's side in 1577 and the brief occupation in 1580 are left out.
- 多聞山城: 織田氏 in 1573–1576 covers the garrisons and 塙直政.
- 信貴山城: no owner is listed for 1542–1559, as the article calls it a gap. 1559 is the traditional date for 松永久秀; some scholars and the article 筒井順慶 give November 1560. Its brief captures in 1563 and 1568 are left out.
- 筒井城: 筒井氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, although the castle fell to 越智氏 or 古市氏 in 1477, 1483 and 1516. The article 松永久秀 says he took it in August 1559, the article 筒井城の戦い that he made no serious attack then; 筒井氏 is listed until 1565, when he drove out 順慶. From 1568 to 1571 it was 松永氏's again.
- 郡山城: owners before 1580 are left out: it was held by the 郡山衆, who changed sides often. 増田氏 is 長盛. 徳川氏 in 1600–1615 is 大久保長安 and other officials, and 筒井氏 placed there by 家康 in 1614–1615. Owners after 1619 are left out.
- 神辺城: 山名氏 is listed from 1443; their lords before 理興 are mostly unknown. 大内氏 in 1549–1552 is the castellan 青景隆著. 杉原氏 is listed from 1552, when 杉原豊後守 went over to 大内氏, following recent research (articles 杉原盛重 and 神辺合戦); older accounts have 山名理興 return in about 1554 and 杉原盛重 follow him in 1557. They served 毛利氏 from 1554. 藤井皓玄's brief capture is left out. 毛利氏 in 1584–1600 is direct rule, then 元康, who moved to 王子山城 in 1598.
- 三原城: 毛利氏 in 1597–1600: 三原 went back to the main house when 隆景 died (article 毛利輝元). 浅野氏 from 1619 are the 三原 branch, chief retainers of 広島藩; the year 1871 is 廃藩置県.
- 岡豊城: 長宗我部氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. It fell in 1508 to 本山氏 and their allies; 1511, when 兼序 came back, follows recent research, and the traditional date is 1518, under 国親.
- 朝倉城: 本山氏 is marked 茂, as 本多氏 has 本 (from 清茂 and 茂辰).
- 宇和島城: 西園寺氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, and covers their retainers 家藤氏 and 西園寺宣久. They gave in to 大友氏 for a time in 1560. 戸田氏 in 1587–1594 is the castellan of 戸田勝隆, whose base was 大洲城. 藤堂氏 in 1600–1608 is the castellan 良勝. 徳川氏 in 1613–1614 is direct rule by the shogunate.
- 中村城: 一条氏 is listed from 1468, when 教房 arrived. Their retainers 為松氏 are said to have built it. 長宗我部氏 in 1574–1600 is first 吉良親貞; later years are inferred, as they held 土佐 until 関ヶ原. 山内氏 (marked 康, from 康豊) is a branch; 1601 is from general knowledge.
- 黒瀬城: 西園寺氏, its only lords, are listed from 1551, the earliest it can have been built. 戸田氏 in 1587–1594 is the castellan 岩城氏; no owner is listed after 戸田勝隆 died.
- 大垣城: owners before 1544 are left out, as sources differ. 斎藤氏 in 1549–1567 covers 竹腰氏 and, from 1559, 氏家氏, their retainers; 氏家氏 went over to 織田氏 in 1567 (from general knowledge). 豊臣氏 in 1585–1590 is 一柳直末, who served 秀次. 伊藤氏 is marked 盛, from 盛景 and 盛宗. 西軍's garrison in 1600 and owners after it are left out.
- 郡上八幡城: 遠藤氏 is marked 慶, from 慶隆. They are listed to 1588, when 稲葉貞通 came; the article says only that 慶隆 was driven out after 本能寺. 1692 and later owners (井上氏, 金森氏, 青山氏) are from general knowledge or left out.
- 岩村城: 遠山氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1572, and includes the months under 織田信長's son and おつやの方. 織田氏 in 1575–1582 is 河尻秀隆, then 団忠正. 森氏 in 1582–1599 is 長可 and 忠政. 田丸氏 is marked 丸, as 田中氏 has 田. Owners after 1600 are left out.
- 若桜鬼ヶ城: 矢部氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period. 尼子氏 in 1575–1576 is 山中幸盛 and the restoration army. Owners in 1576–1581, after 吉川元春 took it, are left out. 木下氏 is 重堅's family, marked 堅, and 山崎氏 is marked 崎.
- 米子城: 山名氏 in 1562–1569 held it with 毛利氏's help. 毛利氏 in 1569–1591 is 吉川元春's castellans 福頼元秀 and 古曳吉種. 中村氏 is the family of 駿府城, 加藤氏 that of 甲府城. 池田氏 from 1617 covers 光政 and 光仲 and their castellans (荒尾氏 from 1632).
- 浜田城: owners after 1648 are left out.
- 備中高松城: 石川氏, retainers of 三村氏, are listed from 1561, when 三村氏 is listed at 備中松山城; who held it before isn't known. 毛利氏 in 1575–1582 is 清水宗治. 宇喜多氏 from 1582 is 花房正成. 花房職秀, from 1600, is left out.
- 新高山城: 小早川氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, although their base was 高山城 until 1552.
- 大内氏館, 高嶺城: 大内氏 in 1551–1557 is 義長. 毛利氏 at 高嶺城 is the castellan 市川経好 and others.
- 若山城: 陶氏, retainers of 大内氏 until 1551 and the power behind them after, are listed from 1470, when 弘護 fortified it.
- 勝山城: 大内氏 is their retainers 内藤氏. No owner is listed after 1557.
- 櫛崎城: 毛利氏 is 内藤隆春, listed from 1557, when 大内氏 fell (that he served 毛利氏 then is from general knowledge), then 毛利秀元.
- 立花山城: 立花氏, a branch of 大友氏, are listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1568, when 立花鑑載 fell after rebelling against 大友氏, and again from 1571, when 戸次鑑連 (道雪) took the name (article 立花道雪). 大友氏 and 毛利氏 in 1568–1571 are left out. 小早川氏 to 1600 includes 秀秋, whose base was 名島城.
- 柳川城: 1581 for 龍造寺氏, who had 蒲池鎮漣 killed, 1587 for 立花宗茂, 1601–1620 for 田中氏 (the family of 岡崎城) and 1620 are from general knowledge.
- 佐賀城: 龍造寺氏 is listed from 1467, the start of the app's period, to 1602. 鍋島直茂 held the real power from 1584. 龍造寺隆信 was driven out by kin in 1551–1553.
- 熊本城: owners before 1550 (出田氏, 鹿子木氏 and 菊池義武) are left out. 城氏 served 大友氏, then 島津氏. 加藤氏 (清正) is a different family from those of 甲府城 and 松山城. 細川氏 here and at 小倉城 is 忠興's line, listed as the same clan as 阿波's.
- 飫肥城: 島津氏 is listed from 1458 and covers their retainers 新納氏 and the 豊州家 branch. 伊東氏 of 日向 is marked 東, as 伊藤氏 is a different family. The owner in 1587–1588 is left out.
- 小倉城: 高橋氏 is 鑑種 and 元種; the 毛利氏 who built it are left out. 毛利氏 (勝信), originally 森氏, isn't the family of 安芸, and is marked 勝. 小笠原氏, from 1632, is left out.
- 知覧城: 島津氏 is their branch 佐多氏, listed from 1467, the start of the app's period.
- 志布志城: 島津氏 in 1467–1562 covers the branches 新納氏 and, from 1538, 豊州家 (articles 新納氏 and 島津忠朝); 1562 is from the article 肝付兼続.
- 佐土原城: 1577 and 1578, when 伊東氏 left and 島津氏 came, are from general knowledge. 徳川氏 in 1600–1603 is 庄田三太夫. 島津氏 from 1603 is 以久's line.
- 延岡城: 高橋氏 is 元種, of 小倉城. Owners after 1613 are left out.
- 忍城: 成田氏 is marked 泰, from 長泰 and 泰季. They sided with 上杉氏 in 1559–1561 and later 後北条氏. 徳川氏 in 1590–1600 is 松平忠吉; that he left in 1600 is from general knowledge, and later owners are left out.
- 本佐倉城: 千葉氏 is listed from 1484. 徳川氏 in 1590–1603 is direct rule, then 松平忠輝. 小笠原吉次 is left out; 土井氏 is listed from 1610.
- Known gaps: 岐阜城 before 1533; 岡崎城 before 1531; 躑躅ヶ崎館 after 1590.
- From general knowledge: 大久保氏 at 小田原城 from 1590; that 田中氏 left 岡崎城 in 1601; and clan readings and English names.
